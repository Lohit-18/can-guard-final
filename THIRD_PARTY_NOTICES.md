## What is *not* third-party

The following are original work in this repository and are covered by the MIT
licence above:

- the vehicle dynamics and driver model (`canguard/vehicle.py`)
- the bus schedule, CAN IDs, cycle times and signal layouts
  (`canguard/config.py`, `canguard/signals.py`)
- the attack implementations (`canguard/attacks.py`)
- the feature set (`canguard/features.py`)
- the model architectures and training procedure (`canguard/models.py`)
- all charts, documentation and generated data

The bus layout and signal scaling are modelled on publicly documented properties
of automotive CAN (ISO 11898, SAE J1979 / OBD-II mode 01) and on published
CAN-IDS literature. They are an illustrative approximation, **not** a
manufacturer's database, and no vendor DBC was consulted or included.
